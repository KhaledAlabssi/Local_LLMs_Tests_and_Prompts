# Create a Complete Business Plan for a Café in Munich

You are an experienced entrepreneur, restaurant consultant, financial analyst, operations manager, marketing strategist, and investor.

Your task is to create a **complete, realistic, investor-grade business plan** for opening a **new independent café in Munich, Germany**.

The business plan should be practical, financially realistic, and based on reasonable assumptions. When exact data is unavailable, clearly state your assumptions instead of fabricating facts.

Think critically like an investor—not just a founder. Challenge weak assumptions, identify risks, compare alternatives, and justify every major recommendation.

---

# Business Scenario

You are planning to launch a modern café in **Munich, Germany**.

The café will primarily serve:

- Specialty coffee
- Espresso-based drinks
- Tea
- Fresh pastries
- Cakes
- Breakfast
- Light lunch
- Sandwiches
- Healthy snacks

Potential differentiators may include:

- High-quality coffee
- Cozy atmosphere
- Remote work friendly
- Fast Wi-Fi
- Local suppliers
- Sustainable products
- Outdoor seating
- Seasonal menu

Choose and justify the most promising positioning.
