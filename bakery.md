# Small Bakery Café Kiosk in Munich

You are an experienced small-business consultant, restaurant operator, financial analyst, and marketing strategist.

Create a **practical mini business plan** for opening a small **bakery and coffee kiosk near a Munich U-Bahn or S-Bahn station**.

The business has a maximum startup budget of **€50,000** and will initially be operated by **2–3 people**.

The goal is to determine whether the concept can realistically work and what would be required to launch it.

---

## 1. Business Concept

Define the concept, including:

* Type of kiosk
* Target customers
* Main products
* Opening hours
* Expected customer experience
* What differentiates the business from nearby bakeries and cafés

Keep the concept realistic for a small space and limited budget.

---

## 2. Target Customers

Identify the most relevant customer groups, such as:

* Commuters
* Office workers
* Students
* Local residents
* Tourists

For each group, explain:

* What they are likely to buy
* When they are likely to visit
* Approximate spending per visit
* Why they would choose this kiosk

---

## 3. Location

Recommend the characteristics of an appropriate location in Munich.

Consider:

* U-Bahn/S-Bahn accessibility
* Foot traffic
* Nearby offices
* Residential areas
* Universities
* Competition
* Rent
* Visibility
* Space requirements

Do not simply name an expensive central location. Consider whether the location makes financial sense for a €50,000 startup budget.

---

## 4. Products & Pricing

Create a small initial menu containing:

### Coffee

* Americano
* Cappuccino

* Other suitable options

### Bakery

* Croissants
* Pretzels
* Muffins
* Cakes or pastries
* Other suitable products

### Optional

* Sandwiches

Provide realistic estimated prices in euros.

Keep the menu small enough for a kiosk with limited staff and storage.

## 5. Startup Budget

Create a realistic startup budget with a maximum of **€50,000**.

Consider:

* Deposit
* Rent before opening
* Renovation
* Coffee machine
* Coffee grinder
* Refrigerator
* Oven/display equipment
* POS system
* Furniture
* Signage
* Initial inventory
* Licenses and permits
* Insurance
* Marketing
* Emergency reserve

Present the budget in a table.

Estimate monthly costs for:

* Rent
* Salaries

* Ingredients
* Packaging

* Software/POS

* Other expenses

## 7. Revenue & Break-Even

Create a simple financial model.

Estimate:

* Average customer spending
* Customers per day
* Daily revenue
* Monthly revenue
* Gross margin
* Monthly operating costs
* Estimated monthly profit/loss
* Break-even number of customers per day

Create **three scenarios**:

1. Conservative
2. Moderate
3. High-demand

Clearly state the assumptions behind each scenario.

Identify which activities should be outsourced or purchased from suppliers rather than performed in-house.

* The biggest opportunity

* The biggest operational challenge

Do **not** blindly assume the business will succeed.

If the numbers do not work under reasonable assumptions, explain what would need to change.
